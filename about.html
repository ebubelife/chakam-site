About Chakam

The story, and how to reach us.

What it is

Chakam reads a chat you already have — a WhatsApp or Telegram export, or screenshots from Instagram, Snapchat, Messenger, or iMessage — and turns it into a set of stats and cards: who texts first, who double-texts, reply speed, red flags, and a short AI-written verdict on the overall dynamic.

Why on-device first

Most of what Chakam shows you is computed directly on your phone. We built it this way on purpose: a chat analyzer that quietly uploads your entire conversation history somewhere is the opposite of what this app is supposed to be. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what does and doesn't leave your device.

Who's behind it

Chakam is built and run by Kodeblooded, an independent app studio. We also build Veyld, an anonymous Q&A app.
