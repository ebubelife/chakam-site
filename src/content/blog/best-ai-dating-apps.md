## What Do AI Dating Apps Actually Do?

Almost every app in this category calls itself an "AI dating assistant", which hides the fact that they do four genuinely different jobs.

- **Write my messages.** You screenshot a chat or a bio, and the app gives you rizz lines and replies to send back.
- **Fix my profile.** The app reviews your bio and photos, or generates new ones.
- **Coach me.** The app works on your habits over time instead of handing you a line.
- **Tell me what is actually happening.** The app reads a conversation you already had and reports the pattern in it.

The first job is by far the most crowded, and it is where the apps are hardest to tell apart. The fourth is nearly empty, which matters more than it sounds, because a line you send is only useful if it works.

## The Best AI Dating Apps, One by One

Every app here is free to download and charges for the useful parts. Apple does not publish subscription prices in a way that can be verified from outside, so read the price screen inside the app before subscribing rather than trusting any figure in an article, this one included.

### RIZZ

[RIZZ](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rizz/id1663430725) is the biggest app in the category by a wide margin. You upload a screenshot of a conversation or a match's bio and it writes replies.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 39,595 ratings, the largest sample on this list.
- **Age rating:** 12+, the lowest here apart from ours.
- **On the store since:** January 2023, last updated January 2026.
- **Watch for:** a 12+ rating on an app built around flirting is worth knowing if you are handing a phone to a teenager.

### WingAI

[WingAI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wingai-your-ai-wingman/id6448704223) does the same core job as RIZZ, aimed explicitly at Tinder, Hinge and Bumble.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 23,575 ratings.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **On the store since:** June 2023, last updated September 2026, the most actively maintained app here.
- **Watch for:** the listing claims "2.5x the reply rate of typical openers". No method is published behind that number, so read it as marketing, not evidence.

### Smoothspeak

[Smoothspeak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smoothspeak-ai-dating-coach/id6739810324) is the one app here built around improvement rather than output. Its listing describes an AI coach called Cue that explains the reasoning behind each suggestion.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 18,449 ratings, a lot for an app released in January 2025.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **Last updated:** August 2026.
- **Watch for:** the listing leans on credentials, citing AI experts from Stanford and Google and insights from top dating coaches. Neither claim is something a buyer can check.

### Sway AI

[Sway AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sway-ai-dating-app-assistant/id6502842382) is a profile app rather than a texting one. It reviews your bio and photos and generates new profile pictures.

- **Rating:** 4.6 from 5,369 ratings.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **Last updated:** June 2025, over a year ago.
- **Watch for:** generated profile photos are the riskiest thing in this whole category. Dating apps verify profiles against a live selfie, so a picture that is not really you creates a problem at verification and a worse one on the first date.

## Why Are There So Many Near-Identical Rizz Apps?

Searching the App Store for "rizz ai dating assistant" returns page after page of apps with nearly the same name, from unrelated sellers, most with fewer than ten ratings. The screenshot-to-reply idea is cheap to build on top of a general AI model, so the category fills with copies faster than anyone can evaluate them.

The practical effect is that ratings stop meaning anything at the bottom of the list. A 5.0 average from four ratings tells you nothing at all. When comparing two apps, look at the number of ratings before you look at the average.

## Which of the Best AI Dating Apps Look Abandoned?

The most useful number on an App Store listing is the one nobody reads: the last update date. WingAI was updated in September 2026 and Smoothspeak in August 2026. Sway AI was last updated in June 2025.

Two once-popular apps look worse. Rz AI Dating Copilot, formerly RizzGPT, holds 2,144 ratings but last shipped in August 2024. YourMove AI Keyboard last shipped in November 2024. An app that has not been updated in two years, in a category defined entirely by which AI model it calls, is not one to hand a subscription to.

## How Do You Use AI Dating Apps Without Being Fake?

The real worry about this category is that you end up sounding like someone else, then have to be that person in real life. Four rules keep it useful:

- **Use it to start, not to sustain.** A generated opener is low cost. Twenty generated messages mean the person is talking to a model, not to you.
- **Edit every line before sending.** If a suggestion does not sound like something you would say, it will not survive a phone call.
- **Do not generate photos that are not you.** This is the one place these apps can actively damage your dating life.
- **Never use them to pressure anyone.** An app that helps you word something clearly is useful. One that helps you wear someone down is not, and that line is easier to cross than it looks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which AI dating app is the best overall?

There is no single best one, because they do different jobs. RIZZ and WingAI write replies, Sway AI works on your profile, and Smoothspeak coaches habits. Chakam is the only one that both writes lines and reads the conversation afterwards, so it suits people who want to know whether any of it is working.

### What is the best rizz app?

RIZZ has the most users by far, so it is the safest pick if all you want is a line. Chakam builds its rizz lines from a match's own profile and bio rather than from a generic template, and it will tell you afterwards whether the conversation actually went anywhere, which no pure rizz app does.

### Are AI dating apps worth paying for?

Some are, if you use them for a specific bottleneck such as opening messages, and stop when it is no longer a bottleneck. They are poor value as a permanent subscription, because the underlying skill does not improve while the app writes for you. Use a free tier long enough to see whether you actually open it.

### Is it cheating to use an AI dating app?

Using one to word an opener is closer to asking a friend for advice than to deception. It becomes dishonest when the person is consistently talking to a model rather than to you, or when generated photos misrepresent your appearance. The test is whether meeting you in person would feel like a surprise.

### Are AI dating apps safe to use with my private chats?

It depends on the app, and most listings are vague about it. A screenshot contains the other person's messages as well as yours, so check what the privacy label says is collected and whether names and contact details are removed before anything is sent. Prefer apps specific enough to tell you.
