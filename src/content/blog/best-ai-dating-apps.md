Search for the best AI dating apps and you get a wall of listicles, most of them written by one of the apps being ranked. So this one uses a different source: each app's own App Store listing, checked on the US store on 26 September 2026. Ratings, release dates and last-update dates all come from there.

One disclosure first. Chakam is our own app, and it is on this list. We have said plainly what it does not do.

## What Do AI Dating Apps Actually Do?

Almost every app in this category gets described as an "AI dating assistant", which hides the fact that they do four genuinely different jobs.

- **Write my messages.** You screenshot a chat or a bio, and the app gives you lines to send back.
- **Fix my profile.** The app reviews your bio and photos, or generates new photos.
- **Coach me.** The app works on your habits over time rather than handing you a line.
- **Explain what happened.** The app reads a conversation you already had and reports the pattern in it.

The first job is by far the most crowded. It is also the one where the apps are hardest to tell apart.

## The Best AI Dating Apps, One by One

Every app here is free to download and charges for the useful parts. Apple does not publish subscription prices in a way that can be checked reliably from outside, so read the price screen inside the app before you subscribe rather than trusting any number in an article, including this one.

### RIZZ

[RIZZ](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rizz/id1663430725) is the biggest app in the category by a wide margin. You upload a screenshot of a conversation or a match's bio and it writes replies.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 39,595 ratings, the largest sample on this list.
- **Age rating:** 12+, the lowest of any app here except ours.
- **On the store since:** January 2023, last updated January 2026.
- **Watch for:** the 12+ age rating on an app built around flirting is worth knowing if you are handing a phone to a teenager.

### WingAI

[WingAI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wingai-your-ai-wingman/id6448704223) does the same core job as RIZZ, aimed explicitly at Tinder, Hinge and Bumble.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 23,575 ratings.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **On the store since:** June 2023, last updated September 2026, which is the most recently maintained app on this list.
- **Watch for:** its listing claims "2.5x the reply rate of typical openers". There is no published method behind that number, so treat it as marketing rather than a finding.

### Smoothspeak

[Smoothspeak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smoothspeak-ai-dating-coach/id6739810324) is the one app here that is built around improvement rather than output. Its listing describes an AI coach called Cue that explains the reasoning behind its suggestions.

- **Rating:** 4.8 from 18,449 ratings, which is a lot for an app released in January 2025.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **Last updated:** August 2026.
- **Watch for:** the listing leans on credentials, saying it was built by AI experts from Stanford and Google and trained on insights from top dating coaches. Neither claim is something a buyer can check.

### Sway AI

[Sway AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sway-ai-dating-app-assistant/id6502842382) is a profile app rather than a texting one. It reviews your bio and photos and generates new profile pictures.

- **Rating:** 4.6 from 5,369 ratings.
- **Age rating:** 17+.
- **Last updated:** June 2025, over a year ago.
- **Watch for:** generated profile photos are the riskiest thing in this category. Dating apps verify profiles against a live selfie, and anything that does not look like you creates a problem at verification and a worse one on the first date.

### Chakam (our app)

[Chakam](/) does the fourth job. You import a chat you have already had, as a WhatsApp or Telegram export or as screenshots, and it reports the pattern: [who is putting in more effort](/blog/one-sided-texting-how-to-know/), reply speed, red and green flags, and [whether interest is fading](/blog/signs-someone-is-losing-interest-texting/).

- **Rating:** none yet. It was released in August 2026 and has no ratings on the US store, which is the honest version of where it stands next to an app with 39,595.

- **Privacy:** analysis runs on your phone. For the AI step, names become anonymous labels and phone numbers, emails, links and handles are removed first, and you can decline that step and still get a full report.
- **Watch for:** it reads a conversation that already happened. It cannot coach you live, and it is not a replacement for an honest conversation.

## Why Are There So Many Near-Identical Rizz Apps?

Searching the App Store for "rizz ai dating assistant" returns page after page of apps with nearly the same name, from unrelated sellers, most with fewer than ten ratings. The screenshot-to-reply idea is cheap to build on top of a general AI model, so the category fills up with copies faster than anyone can evaluate them.

The practical effect is that ratings stop being useful at the bottom of the list. A 5.0 average from four ratings tells you nothing. When comparing two apps here, look at the number of ratings before the average.

## Which of the Best AI Dating Apps Look Abandoned?

The most useful number on an App Store listing is the one nobody reads: the last update date. On this list, WingAI was updated in September 2026 and Smoothspeak in August 2026. Sway AI was last updated in June 2025.

Two once-popular apps look worse. Rz AI Dating Copilot, formerly RizzGPT, holds 2,144 ratings but was last updated in August 2024. YourMove AI Keyboard was last updated in November 2024. An app that has not shipped in two years, in a category defined by which AI model it calls, is not one to hand a subscription to.

## How Do You Use AI Dating Apps Without Being Fake?

The honest worry about this whole category is that you end up sounding like someone else, and then have to be that person in real life. Four rules keep it useful:

- **Use it to start, not to sustain.** A generated opener is low cost. Twenty generated messages mean the person is talking to a model, not to you.
- **Edit every line before sending.** If a suggestion does not sound like something you would say, it will not survive a phone call.
- **Do not generate photos that are not you.** This is the one place these apps can actively damage your dating life.
- **Never use them to pressure anyone.** An app that helps you word something clearly is useful. An app that helps you wear someone down is not, and that line is easier to cross than it looks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which AI dating app is the best overall?

There is no single best one, because they do different jobs. RIZZ and WingAI write replies, Sway AI works on your profile, Smoothspeak coaches habits, and Chakam reads a conversation that already happened. Decide which of those four problems you actually have, then pick within that group by rating count.

### Are AI dating apps worth paying for?

Some are, if you use them for a specific bottleneck such as opening messages, and stop once it is no longer a bottleneck. They are poor value as a permanent subscription, because the underlying skill does not improve while the app writes for you. Try the free tier long enough to see whether you actually open it.

### Is it cheating to use an AI dating app?

Using one to word an opener is closer to asking a friend for advice than to deception. It becomes dishonest when the person on the other end is consistently talking to a model rather than to you, or when generated photos misrepresent what you look like. The test is whether meeting in person would feel like a surprise.

### Can dating apps tell you are using AI?

They can detect generated photos to a degree, and they verify profiles against a live selfie, which is where fabricated pictures fail. Generated text is much harder to detect and is generally not against any rule. The realistic risk is not enforcement, it is the gap between how you text and how you are.

### Are AI dating apps safe to use with my private chats?

It depends entirely on the app, and most listings are vague about it. Screenshots of a conversation contain the other person's messages as well as your own, so before you upload anything, check what the privacy label says is collected and whether names and contact details are removed. Prefer apps that are specific about it.
